🔗 Discord bridge voice + text channels
🔗 Link voice ⛔ Unlink not linked
voice channel ↻ Refresh
Sends this room's voice into your Discord voice channel while you hold it open. Your browser encodes the mix once and the server passes it straight through — it never decodes anything, which is what keeps it inside Render's free tier. Closing the tab or hitting Unlink drops the bot out. Discord voices come back into the room too: they play on your speakers and travel to everyone else over your voice stream. Wear headphones so they do not loop back through your mic.
channel 📥 Relay in 📤 Mirror out
The message genuinely comes from the bot and is labelled with your gamertag — it does not impersonate anyone. Relay in needs the Message Content intent enabled for the bot in Discord's developer portal; without it Discord sends empty message bodies and nothing will appear. Mirror out pushes the room's chat into Discord from this browser only, so only whoever is holding the bridge does the posting and nothing gets doubled up.
